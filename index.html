About A-Z Arcade

A-Z Arcade is a free, browser-based collection of 15 mini-games. It started as a simple A-to-Z typing challenge and has since grown into a full arcade covering typing practice, memory training, reaction-time testing, mental math, classic puzzles (Sliding Puzzle, Simon Says, Word Search, Crossword, Tic-Tac-Toe, 2048, Lights Out and more) and arcade classics like Snake, Whack-a-Mole and Flappy Tap, plus a general knowledge trivia quiz.

Our Mission

The goal of this site is to give anyone with a phone or laptop a quick, ad-supported way to sharpen their typing speed, reflexes, memory and general knowledge — no installation, no sign-up, no cost. Every game runs entirely in your browser.

How We're Funded

This site is supported by Google AdSense advertising. Ads help cover hosting and development time so the games can stay free for everyone to play.

Your Privacy

A-Z Arcade is designed to minimize data collection. We do not intentionally collect or store your personal information or gameplay data on our own servers. Third-party services such as Google AdSense may process certain information as described in our Privacy Policy.

How to Play

Getting Started

From the Home screen, pick a game card — Typing Challenge, Memory Match, Reaction Time, Math Quiz, Puzzles, Arcade or Trivia Quiz. Each game opens its own setup screen where you can choose a mode or difficulty before you start, or tap "Play 100 Levels" (on Typing, Memory, Reaction and Math) to work through a structured level ladder instead of free play.

Typing Challenge Modes Explained

Classic asks you to type A to Z in order. Reverse flips it to Z to A. Random shuffles the alphabet so you never know what's next. Speed is Classic mode on a much shorter timer for an extra challenge. Endless has no timer or word limit — you keep typing random letters until you run out of lives, so it's great for pure stamina practice. Word Rush and Sentences replace single letters with full words or sentences, which is closer to real-world typing. Numbers and Symbols train you on digits and punctuation, which most typing games skip but real typing (passwords, code, forms) needs constantly.

Tips to Improve Your Typing Speed

1. Keep your eyes on the screen, not your fingers — glancing down breaks your rhythm. 2. Start on Classic mode with a longer time limit (45-60s) before moving to Speed mode. 3. Accuracy first, speed second — a wrong keystroke costs more time than typing slightly slower. 4. Practice Numbers and Symbols mode separately since they use different finger positions than letters. 5. Use Endless mode for 5-10 minutes a day to build muscle memory rather than one long session. 6. Track your progress in the Statistics screen — small daily improvements in accuracy matter more than occasional fast runs.

Other Games at a Glance

Memory Match trains short-term visual memory by flipping cards to find pairs. Reaction Time measures how fast you tap after a colour change across 5 rounds. Math Quiz is a timed arithmetic sprint with three difficulty tiers. The Puzzles hub has logic games like Sliding Puzzle, Lights Out, Simon Says, Word Search, Crossword, Tic-Tac-Toe and 2048. The Arcade hub has quick-reflex classics: Snake, Whack-a-Mole and Flappy Tap. Trivia Quiz tests general knowledge across 10 random questions per round.

A Note on Progress

Nothing you do in A-Z Arcade is saved to any server or device storage — your scores and level progress last only for your current browser session and reset when you close or refresh the page.
